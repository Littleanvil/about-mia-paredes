Mia Paredes

Mia Paredes is one of my Original Characters, and I created her as a character for Arcane Odyssey.

About Mia Paredes

Mia Paredes is a sweet ex-princess damsel from who-knows-which-kingdom. From recent events, she has ended up in the Bronze Sea.

Mia Paredes's Interests

Mia Paredes wishes to relearn her past, defeat the Order of Aesir, and bring Beccia, the person who threw her away for prestige, down to her place.

Mia Paredes's Hobbies

Mia Paredes's hobbies include:

  • Potion making and testing
  • Fishing (an acquired taste)
  • Meeting new friends
  • Undisclosed activities that are not endorsed by the Grand Navy
